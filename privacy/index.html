KVM Privacy Policy

Applies to KVM for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Last updated October 22, 2026.

KVM lets you control a computer through a KVM device (such as a NanoKVM) that you own. The developer does not run any server for KVM, does not collect analytics, shows no ads and does not track you.

Your KVM device

Video, keyboard and mouse travel directly between your iPhone, iPad or Mac and your KVM device. They never pass through the developer.

Stored on your devices

  • KVM address and user name, app settings and button layout: stored in the app and synced between your own devices through your iCloud account.
  • KVM password: stored in your Keychain. If you leave sharing on, iCloud Keychain syncs it to your other devices, end-to-end encrypted by Apple.
  • Mac password (optional, for Unlock): stored only in the Keychain of the device where you enter it, readable only after Face ID or your passcode. It is never synced and never written to the app log.
  • App log: kept on your device. It leaves the device only if you choose to share it.

Feature requests

If you post a request, a comment, a vote or a report on the Feature board, that content is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit public database), with the nickname you choose and an anonymous identifier that iCloud assigns to your account for this app. Other KVM users can read requests, comments and nicknames. Only the developer can read reports, and can remove content. You can delete your own comments, and remove your nickname from your requests.

Remote access

Remote access uses Tailscale, built into the app. When you turn it on and sign in, your device connects to Tailscale's coordination servers to reach your KVM. Traffic is end-to-end encrypted. Tailscale's own privacy policy applies to that service.

Purchases

The free trial and KVM Pro are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details.
