KVM Support

KVM for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Contact

Email 723125@gmail.com. To report content on the Feature board, use Report on the request itself, or write to this address.

Supported devices

  • NanoKVM (Sipeed), tested on NanoKVM-Go+.
  • PiKVM, GL.iNet Comet, Geekworm, TinyPilot and BliKVM: supported, not yet tested on real hardware.

Connecting

  • Enter your KVM's address, user name and password, or use Detect to recognize the device type.
  • At home, KVM connects on your local network. Away from home, remote access goes through Tailscale, built into the app.

Purchases

  • 7-day free trial of everything, once per Apple Account. It never renews and charges nothing.
  • After the trial, Direct and Drag control on your home network stay free, with no time limit.
  • KVM Pro is a one-time purchase: joystick, remote access, screen switching and Mac unlock, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Changed device? Use Restore Purchases in Settings.
